# PumaHeart

**Facing matters, the dice decide, and a panicking unit will shove your own line off the board.** A medieval tactics game for two armies, Gold against Green, on a board nine squares wide and eight deep, against the computer or a friend on the same screen.

**Play:** [greykit.com/games/pumaheart](https://greykit.com/games/pumaheart/)

![The AI calling its shot](screenshots/aim.png)

## How it plays

A unit is a base of one to four identical figures, and the figure count is both its strength and its dice pool. You get two actions a turn, chosen from Move, Turn and Attack. Attacks need you next to and facing the target, except for Archers, who fire over your own line, and Heavy Infantry. Each die has three battle-axes, two arrows and one PANIC. If every die an attacker rolls is PANIC, it turns about and retreats, shoving whoever is behind it into panicking too; a retreat that is blocked kills the unit, and chain reactions take your own line with them. Mercenaries never panic, but a King can bribe them across.

Kill the enemy King, or leave it standing alone, to win. Before battle you muster ten bases, deploy them on your two ranks, and both plans are revealed together. The computer plays at three difficulties, and house rules are off by default.

## Keys

Mouse, touch or keyboard, on a desktop or a phone. Click a base to see its moves, or drag it.

| Key | |
|---|---|
| arrows / WASD | move the cursor |
| Enter / Space | select, move or attack |
| Q / E | turn left / right |
| F | attack ahead |
| Z | undo (not back past an attack) |
| . | end turn (press twice) |
| ? / H | help |
| M | mute |

## Saves

Kept in the browser's `localStorage` under keys starting `pumaheart_`. **Settings → Forget everything** clears them.
